    Roadmap to the Platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. No online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission medals — the run is long, not hard. PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap.

      The 100% checklist, in brief

      After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The thresholds are lenient — about 60% true completion. You need 70 of the 80 Challenges; most side quests are not required. The tasks that need everything are Robberies, Underwater Discoveries, Shooting Ranges, Drone Races and Bret's Quests. You only need to scan 200 of 277 base-model vehicles and study 100 of 255 animals through the binoculars. Source: PowerPyx.

      Three rules for the whole run

      • Keep manual saves. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Save at the start of every chapter and once more before the final mission.
      • Do the Chapter 1 meditation. It is the one trophy that can be missed — see the Missables page.
      • Leave cheats off. Turning on a cheat disables trophies (and you cannot save afterwards). Cheats only unlock after the story anyway.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Every story trophy unlocks automatically and cannot be missed, and everything the 100% checklist needs stays reachable in free-roam after the story. Sources: PowerPyx (1 potentially missable) and Push Square (none known), via Planet GTA 6.

      General save advice

      Because there is no mission replay, keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. If you miss the Chapter 1 meditation, that save is your only way back without starting over.
